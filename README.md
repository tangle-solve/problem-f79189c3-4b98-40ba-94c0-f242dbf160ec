### CLIM-000244: Basin-specific marine ice-sheet irreversibility
- **Subfield:** Ice sheets, glaciers, and sea ice
- **Open:** Determine which grounding-line retreats become self-sustaining under realistic buttressing, bed geometry, ocean forcing, and local sea-level feedback. Distinguish temporary retreat from committed ridge crossing.
- **Status:** Idealized instability is established; the 2025 Antarctic assessment emphasizes unresolved basin-specific thresholds and retreat rates.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Separates avoidable ice loss from committed sea-level rise.
- **Key works:** Schoof (2007), [“Ice sheet grounding line dynamics: Steady states, stability, and hysteresis”](https://doi.org/10.1029/2006JF000664); Fricker et al. (2025), [“Antarctica in 2025: Drivers of deep uncertainty in projected ice loss”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adt9619).
